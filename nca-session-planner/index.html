NCA Session Sequence Planner

Writing NCA subjects in the wrong order can cost you months. Some subjects are naturally sequential — Administrative Law before Constitutional Law makes sense conceptually. Others are governed by exam window availability: you cannot write Criminal Law in June because it does not appear in June. This planner optimises your sequence based on the 2026 schedule, difficulty pairing rules, and registration deadlines.
