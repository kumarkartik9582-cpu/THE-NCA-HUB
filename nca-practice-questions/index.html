The IRAC Framework — NCA Exam Structure

I
Issue
Identify the precise legal question raised by the fact pattern.
R
Rule
State the applicable legal test, statute, or principle with the key case.
A
Application
Apply the rule to the specific facts. This is where marks are won or lost.
C
Conclusion
State your answer clearly. The examiner should never have to guess your conclusion.
