Community guidelines

  • Share information that is useful to other candidates — your experience is valuable even if your outcome was a fail.
  • Do not share NCA exam questions or specific exam content — this violates NCA rules and harms everyone.
  • Be specific about your situation: source jurisdiction, how many subjects remain, and what you have already tried.
  • Verify official information (fees, dates, policy) directly at nca.legal before acting on community advice.
  • No promotional content without disclosure. No solicitation.
