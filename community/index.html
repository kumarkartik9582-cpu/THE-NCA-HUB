Community Standards

Community Rules

This community only works if everyone contributes. Five rules, no exceptions.

  • 1. Be generous with knowledge. Share resources, summarise cases, post practice questions. The more you give, the more you get.
  • 2. No spam, no self-promotion. This is a study community, not a marketplace. Commercial posts will be removed immediately.
  • 3. Respect the process. Everyone is at a different stage. No gatekeeping, no condescension. We all started somewhere.
  • 4. Do not share exam content. Sharing actual NCA exam questions or answers is strictly prohibited and may violate NCA confidentiality policies.
  • 5. Verify before sharing. Always cite your source. Outdated information in this community can genuinely harm someone's preparation. When in doubt, link to nca.legal directly.
