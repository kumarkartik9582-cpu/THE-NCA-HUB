## The Working Professional Calculation

**Scenario:** Working full-time, 2 hours weekdays, 4–5 hours weekends

- Weekly hours: 18–20
- Monthly hours: 72–80
- 3-month total: 216–240 hours

**This is sufficient for:**
- 2–3 subjects at recommended level
- 3–4 subjects at minimum level

---

## The Compressed Timeline

**Scenario:** One month, 3–4 hours daily

- Monthly hours: 90–120

**This is sufficient for:**
- Administrative Law (with intensive focus)
- Professional Responsibility (with practice experience)

**This is tight for:**
- Constitutional Law
- Foundations of Canadian Law

---

## Quality Over Quantity

Two focused hours with strategic notes beats four hours with comprehensive textbooks.

**Efficient study:**
- Condensed notes (under 80 pages)
- Daily timed practice
- Framework drilling
- Mock exams

**Inefficient study:**
- Extensive reading
- Passive video watching
- No timed practice
- No exam conditioning
