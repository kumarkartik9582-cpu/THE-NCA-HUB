This guide explains the entire system — what the NCA is, how the exams work, and how candidates typically prepare. --- ## What is the NCA? The National Committee on Accreditation evaluates the legal education of internationally trained lawyers to determine whether it is equivalent to a Canadian law degree. Candidates who do not meet equivalency requirements must complete **NCA exams or assigned courses**. --- ## NCA Required Subjects Most candidates are assigned five core subjects: - Administrative Law - Constitutional Law - Criminal Law - Professional Responsibility - Foundations of Canadian Law For a detailed breakdown of how the exams work, see: [NCA Exam Format Explained →](/blog/article-a2-nca-exam-format/) --- ## How Difficult Are the NCA Exams?
