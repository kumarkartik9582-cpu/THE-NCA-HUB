# Backlink, Outreach & Review Kit — The NCA Hub

Once the on-page work is done (it is), the two things that move Google rankings and sales the most are **backlinks from relevant sites** and **more real reviews**. Both are off-page and need a human to send messages, so this kit is copy-paste ready. Work top to bottom; 30 minutes a week compounds.

**Ground rules:** every message is honest and personal. Never buy links. Never fake reviews — only ask real buyers. Do not name or disparage competitors.

---

## PART 1 — Backlinks (authority that lifts rankings)

### Tier 1 — Directories & listings (fast, do this week)
Submit The NCA Hub to:
- Legal-education and study-resource directories
- Canadian immigration / newcomer resource lists
- "Tools for internationally trained professionals" roundups
- Product directories (e.g., education tools, study aids)
- Your Google Business Profile (see `GOOGLE_BUSINESS_PROFILE_SETUP.md`)

Each is a 5-minute form. Aim for 10 in week one.

### Tier 2 — Communities where NCA candidates already gather (highest-converting traffic AND links)
Be genuinely helpful first; link only when it answers the question.
- **Reddit:** r/NCA, r/LawCanada, r/immigration, r/ontario (search "NCA" threads). Answer questions thoroughly; mention the free readiness score / free chapter when relevant.
- **Facebook groups:** "NCA Exam Candidates", "Internationally Trained Lawyers in Canada", country-specific lawyer-in-Canada groups (Indian/Nigerian/Pakistani lawyers in Canada).
- **WhatsApp/Telegram** NCA cohorts (you already have community reach — seed the free readiness score there).
- **Quora:** answer "How do I prepare for the NCA exams?", "Are NCA notes worth it?", "How much does the NCA cost?"

### Tier 3 — Referral partners & guest posts (slower, high authority)
- **Law school international-student / graduate offices** (Osgoode, U of T, UBC, Queen's, etc.) — they field NCA questions constantly and will happily link a free readiness tool + guides.
- **Immigration lawyers & consultants** who serve internationally trained lawyers — offer them the free tools to share with clients.
- **Guest posts** on legal-career and immigration blogs — offer an original piece (e.g., "What internationally trained lawyers get wrong about the NCA exams").

## PART 2 — Reviews (your single biggest conversion gap)

You have one public testimonial (Anum). Social proof is what turns hesitant visitors into buyers. Every buyer is a potential review — ask systematically.

### The ask (send 3–5 days after purchase, once they've used the notes)
> Subject: Quick favour — how are the [Subject] notes working out?
> Hi [Name], thanks for getting the [Subject] notes. Two things:
> 1) If anything's unclear, reply here — I read every message personally.
> 2) If they're helping, would you write one or two lines about your experience? A short quote (first name + subject + attempt is perfect) helps other candidates decide, and I may feature it on the site. Just reply to this email.
> Either way, good luck on exam day. — Kartik

### After they pass (the gold review)
> Subject: Congratulations!! 🎉
> Hi [Name], amazing news. If you're open to it, a sentence on what worked — especially if you'd failed before or were short on time — would genuinely help the next candidate in your shoes. With your permission I'll add it (first name + subject) to the site.

### Where to route reviews
- On-site testimonial (feature the strongest, like Anum's).
- **Google Business Profile** reviews (see that doc) — these show in Google Search and Maps and drive trust + local SEO.
- Screenshots (with permission) for Meta/Instagram ad creative.

### Make it easy
- Add a one-line P.S. to the Payhip delivery email: "Loved the notes? Reply with a sentence — it helps other candidates."
- Keep a running file of quotes (name, subject, attempt, result, permission Y/N) so they're ready to add to the site. Send me new confirmed ones and I'll place them.
