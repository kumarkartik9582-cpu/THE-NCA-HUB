# Outreach Target List — real, specific places to earn backlinks & reach

This removes the "who do I even email?" friction. Every entry below is a **real, verified page** (checked Aug 2026). Pair it with the email templates in `READY_TO_SEND_ASSETS.md` (§1) and the guest posts in that file and `GROWTH_CONTENT_PACK_2.md`.

**How to use:** for each target, open the URL, find the right contact (usually a "contact us", program admin, or author email on the page), and send the matching template. Aim for 3–5 sends a week. Do not mass-blast — personalise the first line to each.

---

## TIER 1 — Canadian law schools with NCA / internationally-trained-lawyer programs
These offices field NCA questions constantly and many keep a "resources" or "useful links" section. Use **email 1A** (university office) + attach the press one-pager. Ask them to link your free tools (exam schedule, readiness score).

## TIER 3 — Blogs & newsletters in this exact niche (guest-post targets)
Use **email 1C** + offer a guest post (§2 of READY_TO_SEND_ASSETS.md or the second one in GROWTH_CONTENT_PACK_2.md). These already write for internationally trained lawyers.
- **The Authentic Lawyer** (Substack) — writes first-hand about the ITL licensing journey. Perfect fit for your "how I passed" guest post. https://authenticlawyer.substack.com/ (comment genuinely on a post first, then pitch.)
- Search for more: Google `"internationally trained lawyer" Canada blog`, `NCA exam blog`, `"foreign trained lawyer" Canada Substack`. Pitch each the guest post.

## TIER 4 — Communities (traffic + natural links; be helpful first)
- **Reddit:** r/NCAExams (your warmest audience — post the schedule piece), plus r/LawCanada and r/immigration for relevant threads. Search each for "NCA" and answer questions thoroughly.
- **Facebook:** search "NCA Exam Candidates", "Internationally Trained Lawyers in Canada", and country-specific groups ("Indian lawyers in Canada", "Nigerian lawyers in Canada"). Join, be helpful, share the free tools when they answer a question.
- **WhatsApp/Telegram** NCA cohorts you're already in — seed the free readiness score and the schedule link.
- **Quora:** answer "How do I prepare for the NCA exams?", "How much does the NCA cost?", "Are NCA notes worth it?" — link the relevant free guide in each answer.

## TIER 5 — Directories & listings (fast, 5-min forms)
- Your **Google Business Profile** (see `GOOGLE_BUSINESS_PROFILE_SETUP.md`) — do this first; it also unlocks Google reviews.
- Education / study-resource directories, "tools for newcomers to Canada" lists, legal-tech and study-aid roundups. Search: `submit "study resources" directory`, `"internationally trained professionals" Canada resources list`.
