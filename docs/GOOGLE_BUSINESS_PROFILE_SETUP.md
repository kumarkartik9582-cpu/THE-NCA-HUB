# Google Business Profile — Setup Guide

**Time required:** 15 minutes
**Expected impact:** Improved local search presence, Knowledge Panel in Google results

---

## Step-by-Step Setup

### 1. Go to Google Business Profile
- Open [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
- Sign in with the Google account you want to manage the listing

### 2. Add Your Business
- Click **"Add your business to Google"** or **"Manage now"**
- Business name: **The NCA Hub**

### 3. Choose Your Category
- Primary category: **Educational Institution** or **Tutoring Service**
- If available, also add: **Test Preparation Center**

### 4. Set Service Area
- Select **"Yes"** for "Do you serve customers outside this location?"
- Service area: **Canada** (nationwide — online service)
- Do NOT set a physical address (this is an online business)

### 5. Add Contact Information
- Website: **https://www.thencahub.com**
- Phone: Add if you have a business phone (optional)
- Email: Your business email

### 6. Write the Business Description
Paste this:

```
NCA exam study notes and tools for internationally trained lawyers qualifying in Canada. Covering all 5 mandatory NCA subjects: Administrative Law, Constitutional Law, Criminal Law, Foundations of Canadian Law, and Professional Responsibility. Under 80 pages per subject, built for the open-book, 3-hour exam format. Used by candidates from 12+ countries.
```

### 7. Add Business Hours
- Set as: **Available 24/7** (online resource)
- Or set response hours: **Monday–Friday, 9 AM – 6 PM EST**

### 8. Add Photos
- Upload your og-image or logo
- Add a screenshot of the notes index page

### 9. Verify Your Business
- Google will offer verification methods (email, phone, or postcard)
- Email or phone verification is fastest
- Verification may take 1–7 days

### 10. Post-Verification
Once verified:
- Add **Products**: List each subject's notes as a product ($100 CAD each)
- Add **Posts**: Share exam date reminders, new articles
- Enable **Messaging** if you want direct inquiries
- Add a **Q&A** section with common NCA questions

---

## Why This Matters
- A verified Google Business Profile creates a Knowledge Panel when people search "The NCA Hub"
- It builds trust with candidates who are evaluating the site
- It provides another channel for reviews (ask Anum to leave one)
- Google Business listings can appear in local packs even for online businesses
