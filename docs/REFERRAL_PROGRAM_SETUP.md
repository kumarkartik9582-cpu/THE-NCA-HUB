# Payhip Referral/Affiliate Program Setup Guide

**Time required:** 10 minutes
**Expected impact:** $25 per referred sale, leveraging existing WhatsApp community

---

## Step-by-Step Setup

### 1. Enable the Affiliate Program

1. Log in to [payhip.com/THENCAHUB](https://payhip.com/THENCAHUB)
2. Go to **Dashboard** > **Marketing** > **Affiliates** (or **Referral Program**)
3. Toggle the affiliate program **ON**

### 2. Configure Commission Settings

- **Commission type:** Fixed amount
- **Commission amount:** $25 CAD per sale
- Alternatively: **Percentage** at ~14% (which equals ~$25 on a $175 sale)
- **Cookie duration:** 30 days (recommended — gives referrers credit if the buyer returns within 30 days)
- **Auto-approve affiliates:** ON (makes it easy for candidates to join)

### 3. Set Up Payout Rules

- **Minimum payout:** $50 CAD (requires 2 referrals before payout)
- **Payment method:** PayPal (Payhip default)
- **Payout frequency:** Monthly

### 4. Create Affiliate Welcome Message

When someone signs up as an affiliate, they should see:

```
Welcome to the NCA Hub Referral Program!

You earn $25 CAD for every colleague who purchases NCA study notes through your link.

How it works:
1. Share your unique referral link in WhatsApp groups, social media, or directly with colleagues
2. When someone purchases through your link, you earn $25
3. Payouts are monthly via PayPal (minimum $50)

Your referral link is shown below. Copy it and share!

Tips for sharing:
- Share in NCA WhatsApp groups when someone asks about study materials
- Post in NCA-related Facebook groups
- Share with colleagues from your home country who are starting the NCA process
```

### 5. Monitor and Manage

Check these weekly:
- **Affiliate signups** — how many people joined
- **Clicks** — how many people clicked referral links
- **Conversions** — how many purchased through referral links
- **Payouts** — upcoming and completed payouts

### 6. Promote the Program

- Add "Refer a Colleague" link to the website footer
- Mention in post-purchase thank-you emails
- Share in your own WhatsApp groups
- Add a note on the /nca-passes/ success wall page
