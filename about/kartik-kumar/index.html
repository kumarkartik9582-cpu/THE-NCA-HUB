Why I Built This

When I started the NCA process, I looked for study materials that would help me pass efficiently. What I found instead were 400-page textbooks written for Canadian law students — comprehensive, but not designed for someone who already had a law degree and needed to learn Canadian-specific content for an exam. The materials were too long, too generic, and had no answer templates or exam-focused structure.

I decided to take a different approach to my own preparation. Instead of reading entire textbooks, I distilled each subject down to its exam-testable core — the rules, frameworks, and landmark cases that actually appear on NCA exams. I created my own condensed notes with structured answer templates for every question type. The result: I cleared 4 of 5 mandatory subjects in under 3 months.

After passing, I realised that every internationally trained lawyer going through the NCA process faces the same problem — too much material, not enough exam focus. The NCA Hub exists to solve that. Every set of notes is under 80 pages, built around answer templates, and designed specifically for the NCA exam format — not for a Canadian law school course.

I built what I wished had existed when I started. If you are an internationally trained lawyer preparing for the NCA, these notes are designed to get you through efficiently — without wasting months on material that will not appear on your exam.
