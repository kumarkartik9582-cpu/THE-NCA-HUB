FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Administrative Law the hardest NCA subject?

Administrative Law and Foundations of Canadian Law are consistently rated the most challenging. Vavilov has no direct equivalent in most foreign legal systems. Candidates who prepare specifically for Vavilov and Baker tend to find the exam more manageable.

How long should I study for NCA Administrative Law?

Most candidates require 100-140 study hours for Administrative Law. Candidates with some administrative law background may need less.

Is Administrative Law open-book?

Yes. All NCA exams are open-book — hard copy materials only. No digital notes, no internet access during the exam.
