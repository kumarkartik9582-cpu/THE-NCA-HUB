NCA Exam Statistics 2026

Key figures on NCA assessment volumes, exam fees, pass rates, and candidate demographics — compiled from official NCA publications and the Law Society of Ontario annual reports.

Data sourcing note: Statistics are drawn from publicly available sources including nca.legal, the Federation of Law Societies of Canada, and Law Society annual reports. Where exact figures are unavailable, ranges are provided with source citations. The NCA does not publish granular subject-level pass rates publicly.

At a Glance — 12 Key Numbers

~1,200
Applications per year
Approximate annual NCA applications from internationally trained lawyers. Figures vary by year; the NCA does not publish granular annual statistics. Source: nca.legal
130+
Source countries
Countries from which applicants regularly apply to the NCA for accreditation assessment
5
Mandatory subjects
All applicants must pass: Administrative Law, Constitutional Law, Criminal Law, Foundations of Canadian Law, and Professional Responsibility
$400
Initial assessment fee (CAD)
Fee for the NCA to review your credentials and issue a Statement of Qualifications. Effective March 1, 2024. Source: nca.legal
$500
Per exam attempt (CAD)
Cost per NCA challenge exam, payable to the NCA regardless of pass or fail outcome. Source: nca.legal
4×
Maximum exam attempts
A candidate may attempt any single NCA exam no more than 4 times total. After the 4th fail, re-assessment is required. Source: nca.legal
3 hrs
Exam duration
Every NCA challenge exam runs for exactly 3 hours. Open-book format; candidates may bring any printed or written materials.
~12
Exam sessions per year
NCA exams are offered approximately monthly across the year. Different subjects are scheduled across different months. Full schedule at nca.legal
~60%
Estimated first-attempt pass rate
The NCA does not publish official pass rates by subject. Based on candidate community reports and law school admission data, estimated at 55–65% for prepared candidates.
12–18 mo
Typical completion timeline
Average time from initial assessment to Certificate of Qualification for full-requirement candidates attempting exams one session at a time
India & UK
Top source jurisdictions
Lawyers from India and the United Kingdom represent the largest share of NCA applicants, followed by Nigeria, Pakistan, and the Philippines
Mar 2026
New requirements effective
From March 1, 2026: all new applicants must pass a language screening (Versant VEPT, min. score 61) AND demonstrate Indigenous Law and Peoples competency before assessment. Source: nca.legal

NCA Exam Fees — Full Breakdown (2025–2026)

Source: nca.legal — official NCA fee schedule effective 2025–2026. All amounts in CAD.

Item Fee (CAD) Notes
Initial credentials assessment $400 One-time fee to receive Statement of Qualifications. Effective March 1, 2024.
Re-assessment (if required) $400 If candidate fails all attempts on a subject; new assessment may result in different requirement
NCA challenge exam — per subject $500 Applies to each attempt regardless of result
5 exams (all mandatory subjects) $2,500 If all passed first attempt; total with assessment = $3,350
Elective subject exam $500 Same fee; required if NCA requires electives (e.g. Contracts, Civil Procedure)
Certificate of Qualification $0 Issued at no charge once all requirements are met
Law Society admission (Ontario — 2026) ~$3,000–$5,000+ Separate LSBC/LSO licensing fees; varies by province and route (articling vs. LPP)
