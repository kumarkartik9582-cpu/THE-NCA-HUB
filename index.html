The NCA Hub — NCA Exam Preparation for Internationally Trained Lawyers in Canada

The NCA Hub provides concise study notes, answer templates, a readiness assessment, and preparation guidance for the National Committee on Accreditation (NCA) challenge examinations in Canada. Required for internationally trained lawyers — including those with Indian LLB degrees, UK law degrees, Nigerian law degrees, and qualifications from other jurisdictions — who wish to practise law in Canada.

The Founder's Story

Three UK law firms.
A Supreme Court judgment.
