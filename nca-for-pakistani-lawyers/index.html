What subjects do Pakistani lawyers need to write?

Pakistani lawyers who qualified under the Pakistan Bar Council are assessed as common law trained (Pakistan inherited a common law system from British rule). The NCA typically assigns 5 to 7 NCA challenge exams — the same general range as Indian lawyers given the shared common law heritage.

Pakistan law vs Canadian law — key differences

  • Administrative law: Pakistan's administrative law follows a judicial review framework influenced by English law but without the structured Vavilov/Dunsmuir standard of review analysis.
  • Constitutional law: The Constitution of Pakistan 1973 includes fundamental rights provisions, but the Charter of Rights and Freedoms analysis (s.1 Oakes test, s.32 threshold, s.33 notwithstanding clause) differs substantially.
  • Criminal law: The Pakistan Penal Code 1860 shares English common law origins with the Canadian Criminal Code but differs significantly in specific offences, defences, and sentencing.
  • Foundations of Canadian Law: The bijural nature of Canadian law (common law + Quebec Civil Code) and the modern principle of statutory interpretation (Driedger) are unique to Canada.

Timeline for Pakistani lawyers qualifying in Canada

  • NCA assessment: 8–16 weeks
  • NCA exams (5–7 subjects): 1.5–3 years
  • Indigenous Law competency: Alongside exams
  • COQ + articling + bar: 12–16 months

Total: 3–5 years from NCA application to call.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many NCA exams do Pakistani lawyers typically need?

Pakistani lawyers are typically assigned 5 to 7 NCA challenge exams. All five mandatory subjects are usually required. Some candidates are also assigned Property Law and/or Contracts depending on their specific transcripts and syllabi.

Is a Pakistan Bar Council licence recognised by the NCA?

The NCA assesses your law degree (LLB) and legal training, not the Bar Council licence directly. Your law school transcripts and syllabi are the primary evidence of your legal education for the NCA assessment.

Can Pakistani lawyers write NCA exams from Pakistan?

Yes. NCA exams are online-proctored via MonitorEDU and can be written from anywhere in the world with a stable internet connection, working webcam, and microphone.
