How to Use This Schedule

→Study each subject intensively for its assigned weeks before moving to the next. Do not spread subjects across weeks.
→Mock exam weeks: simulate real exam conditions. Use your notes hub practice questions. Review every wrong answer.
→Buffer weeks are for catch-up or deeper review on subjects you found most challenging.
→Final prep week: do not start new material. Focus on quick-reference summaries and past exam questions.
→If working full time, protect your weekend study blocks fiercely. Even 3 focused hours on Saturday and Sunday adds up.
