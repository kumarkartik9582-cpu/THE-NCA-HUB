Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which cookies The NCA Hub uses, and how you can control them. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember information about your visit — such as your preferences and browsing patterns — making your next visit easier and the site more useful to you.

In addition to traditional cookies, we also use browser storage technologies such as localStorage and sessionStorage, which function similarly but store data locally in your browser rather than sending it to a server with every request.

3. Google Analytics and Consent Mode

We implement Google Analytics 4 with Consent Mode. By default, analytics storage is set to denied when you first visit the site. Google Analytics only activates data collection after you explicitly accept cookies via our consent banner.

If you decline cookies, Google Analytics will not collect your data. Cloudflare security cookies will still be set as they are strictly necessary for the site to operate.

4. Your Rights Under GDPR and CASL

Depending on where you are located, you may have the following rights regarding cookies and the personal data associated with them:

  • Right to be informed — You have the right to know what data we collect and why (this policy).
  • Right to withdraw consent — You may withdraw your consent to analytics cookies at any time by clearing your browser storage or using the opt-out methods below.
  • Right of access — You may request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to erasure — You may request that we delete any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to object — You may object to the processing of your data for analytics purposes.

Under Canada's CASL (Canadian Anti-Spam Legislation), we obtain your express or implied consent before collecting personal information via tracking technologies where required.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at thencahub@gmail.com.

5. How to Opt Out

Browser Settings

You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies at any time. Note that blocking all cookies may affect site functionality. Instructions for major browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Google Analytics Opt-Out

To opt out of Google Analytics specifically across all websites, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. This prevents Google Analytics from collecting data about your visits.

Clearing localStorage

To clear locally stored reading history and consent preferences, open your browser's developer tools (F12), navigate to Application > Local Storage, and delete entries beginning with nca_ or cookie-consent. Alternatively, clearing your browser's site data will remove all localStorage entries for this site.
